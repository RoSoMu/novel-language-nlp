# Raw Conversational Corpus

This directory contains the original conversational excerpts
collected for the Novel Language in NLP project.

The source material includes authentic exchanges involving
creative lexical formations, morphological variation,
metaphorical extensions, and locally established vocabulary.

## Data provenance

Source: Extended human–AI conversations concerning the
reconstruction and statistical analysis of a psycholinguistic
experiment.

Initial collection: 10 selected conversational exchanges.

Selection: Purposive, exploratory sampling of interactions
involving the Frankenstein lexical family.

## Privacy and publication

Original conversational records are excluded from version control.

The raw corpus may contain informal language, personal references,
and material unsuitable for public distribution.

Only reviewed and appropriately edited or selected excerpts
will be included in public datasets.

Edited excerpts must be identified as edited and must not be
presented as verbatim original observations.
